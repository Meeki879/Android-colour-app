## 2. What this means for us

1. **"No ads, no data" is expected, not special.** Several indie toddler apps already claim it (group A). It stays in the product but cannot be the headline.
2. **The pain is real.** Ad-driven exits and upsells in group B are what parents complain about. Our parental gate and no external links in the child's area answer it directly.
3. **The gap is the combination.** Group D has the visual quality but is not built for toddlers or sensory regulation. Groups A and C are built for children but use simple effects. Nothing found in this search combines fluid-class visuals, toddler-proofing and sensory tuning.
4. **Features not found in any listing searched:** a gentle wind-down timer, placeable objects that redirect effects, explicit photosensitivity limits. "Not found" is not proof of absence; check before claiming "first".
5. **Price anchor:** free and one-time-unlock apps are plentiful. Free is right for adoption.
6. **Closest threats:** Sensory Light Box (same market, cross-platform) and the fluid simulators (visual quality).

## 3. Revised value proposition

Before: "Calm, safe light play that answers every touch. No ads, no data, no tricks."

After: "Fluid-quality light play tuned for sensory regulation: calm by default, toddler-proof, never strobes, ends gently."
